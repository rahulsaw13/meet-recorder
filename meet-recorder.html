Meet Recorder

Records the screen, system audio (other people) and your mic into one file.

00:00:00 
Ready.
  1. Click Start recording.
  2. In the share dialog choose Entire screen and tick "Also share system audio". Without it, other people's voices are not recorded.
  3. Switch to Meet. Recording keeps going in the background.
  4. Come back and click Stop, or use Chrome's "Stop sharing" bar.
